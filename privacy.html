1. Introduction & Core Privacy Philosophy

At Navarambha Labs, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Netra was engineered from the ground up on a simple, non-negotiable principle:

Your network traffic belongs to you. Netra processes it on your device and sends it only where you direct it. We do not log or collect your browsing activity.

This Privacy Policy explains how Netra handles information when you use our Android application. Unlike traditional commercial VPN operators, Netra is a client-side proxy utility and privacy tunnel. We do not operate centralized tracking servers, proxy relays, data harvesting infrastructure, or advertising networks.

2. Information We DO NOT Collect

Netra does NOT collect, monitor, log, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • ❌ No Browsing History: We do not log web pages visited, searches conducted, or applications accessed.
  • ❌ No Traffic Payloads: Packet data traversing the local tunnel is never inspected, recorded, or mirrored.
  • ❌ No DNS Query Logs: Domain name lookups intercepted by Netra's on-device DNS engine are resolved in-memory and immediately discarded.
  • ❌ No Connection Timestamps or IP Addresses: We do not log when you connect, when you disconnect, or your originating/destination IP addresses.
  • ❌ No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): Netra requires no user account, email address or phone number. Netra Pro payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.
  • ❌ No Device Fingerprinting: Netra's own code does not read or send IMEI, MAC addresses, Android Advertising IDs (AAID), Android ID or other hardware identifiers. Google Play libraries included in the app (Google Play Billing and In-App Updates) may collect device identifiers and send them to Google; see Section 6.

3. How Netra Uses Android's VpnService API

Netra utilizes Android’s official VpnService API to establish a local, virtual network interface (tun0) on your Android device.

Why VpnService is Required:

  1. Local Traffic Capture & Routing: VpnService allows Netra to intercept outbound Layer-3 IP packets directly on your device and route them through user-configured proxy servers (SOCKS5, HTTP CONNECT, or Shadowsocks).
  2. On-Device Threat & Tracker Filtering: The local tunnel intercepts UDP port 53 DNS queries to evaluate requested domains against locally stored threat definitions. Matching tracker or advertising domains are blackholed locally to 0.0.0.0 before unwanted connections can leave your device.

Key Architectural Disclosures:

  • On-Device Processing: Packet processing, Fake-IP synthesis and domain filtering happen in your device's memory.
  • No Routing Through Us: Navarambha Labs runs no proxy servers. In "Direct" mode traffic flows directly from your device to its destination via your network provider.
  • DNS: Traffic sent through your proxy has its DNS resolved remotely by the proxy. Direct traffic uses DNS-over-HTTPS (Cloudflare by default; Google or Quad9 with Pro), falling back to your network's normal DNS if DoH is unreachable or cannot resolve a name (for example an intranet name). The hostname of your proxy server itself is always looked up with your network's normal DNS.
  • Encryption: SOCKS5 and HTTP proxies do not encrypt traffic between your device and the proxy. Shadowsocks (Netra Pro) does.
  • User-Defined Relays: Traffic routed through proxies is transmitted only to the specific servers, hostnames, and ports that you explicitly configure or import.

4. Local Storage & Hardware-Backed Credential Security

All configuration data entered into Netra remains strictly on your local device:

  • Keystore-Backed Credential Encryption: Proxy credentials are encrypted using AES-256-GCM via AndroidX EncryptedSharedPreferences, with keys held by the Android Keystore. If a device can't provide this, passwords are kept in memory only (never saved unencrypted) and the app tells you.
  • Cloud Backup Exclusion: Credential stores, the encrypted subscription cache, update/security state and the list of apps you exclude from the tunnel (split tunnelling) are excluded from Android Auto Backup and device transfers. Versions 1.0.5 and earlier included the split-tunnelling app list in Android backup; from version 1.0.6 it stays on your device.
  • Android Backup of Other Settings: If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may back up Netra's other settings (such as routing rules, allowlisted domains, DNS provider and theme) to your Google account. This backup is performed and stored by Android and Google; it is never sent to us.
  • Encrypted Manual Backups (Pro): Exports are always encrypted with a passphrase you choose (AES-256-GCM, PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256). The passphrase is never stored; temporary share files are deleted afterwards.

5. Android Permissions & Justifications

Netra requests only the minimal permissions required to function as an on-device network proxy utility:

Permission Identifier Status Purpose
VPN Service BIND_VPN_SERVICE Core Establishes the local TUN interface for on-device packet filtering and proxy routing.
Internet Access INTERNET Required Transmits outbound traffic to user-configured proxy servers and updates community rule definitions.
Network State ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Required Monitors Wi-Fi and Cellular network transitions to prevent dropped sockets.
Change Network State CHANGE_NETWORK_STATE Required Allows socket protection to bypass the VPN tunnel for proxy outbound connections (VpnService.protect()).
Foreground Service FOREGROUND_SERVICE Required Keeps the packet processing engine alive in the background while other apps are active.
Notifications POST_NOTIFICATIONS Optional Displays a persistent notification showing active tunnel state and live uplink/downlink speeds. Requested the first time you connect.
Query All Packages QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES Optional Lists installed apps for split tunnelling. The installed-app list is only shown on your device; the apps you choose to exclude are saved on your device, never sent to us, and (from version 1.0.6) not included in Android backup.
Google Play Billing com.android.vending.BILLING Optional Buy and restore a Netra Pro subscription through Google Play.

6. Network Requests Netra Makes

None of these requests contain your browsing history, DNS history or traffic contents, but like any network request they reveal your IP address to the service contacted.

  1. Your Proxy Servers: Traffic routed through SOCKS5, HTTP CONNECT or Shadowsocks goes only to the servers you configure. Subscription links you import (https only) are downloaded from the address you paste.
  2. DNS-over-HTTPS: DNS for direct traffic goes to Cloudflare by default (Google or Quad9 if you choose them), with your network's DNS as fallback (also used for names DoH can't resolve and for your proxy server's own hostname). Those providers' privacy policies apply.
  3. Update Check: On launch and when the app returns to the foreground, Netra downloads a small version file from our website (https://nborkar88.github.io/netra-site/app-version.json, hosted on GitHub Pages) to learn whether this version is still supported. No identifiers are sent; GitHub may log the request under its privacy statement.
  4. Google Play Services: Google Play In-App Updates (to offer updates) and Google Play Billing (prices, purchase, verification and restore of Netra Pro). These libraries are provided by Google and may collect information about your device, including device or other identifiers, and send it to Google to provide these services. Google's Privacy Policy applies to that data. We only receive the subscription status Google Play reports.
  5. Blocklist Updates (Pro): When you tap "Update Threat Definitions Now", Netra downloads plain-text rule files from public repositories on GitHub (such as StevenBlack unified hosts). No user data is sent.

7. Global Privacy Regulations Compliance

GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation - EU)

Netra does not send your browsing data to us, and we operate no servers that store personal data about you. Subscription purchases are processed by Google as described in Google's Privacy Policy.

CCPA / CPRA (California Consumer Privacy Act)

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not collect personal information that we could disclose or delete.

COPPA (Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act)

Netra is a technical network utility and is not directed towards children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
