VpnService Declaration & Google Play Policy Alignment

Total transparency on how Netra handles your network traffic and why system permissions are used.

Why Netra Utilizes Android's VpnService API

Android requires apps that inspect or route Layer-3 network packets to use the official VpnService framework. Netra operates as a client-side local tunnel utility rather than a commercial surveillance VPN:

🏠

Local Device Loopback

Packets processed by Netra's TUN interface are filtered and evaluated entirely in local device memory. No data is sent to central company servers.

🚫

Strict Zero-Log Architecture

Netra never logs, stores, or transmits browsing history, DNS lookups, app usage, or payload packets. No telemetry SDKs are included in the app.

🔐

Hardware Keystore Protection

Sensitive proxy passwords and Shadowsocks keys are encrypted via AES-256-GCM and stored directly in the hardware-backed Android Keystore.

Android System Permissions Breakdown

Permission System Identifier Requirement Purpose
VPN Service BIND_VPN_SERVICE Mandatory Instantiates local TUN interface for on-device threat filtering and proxy socket routing.
Internet Access INTERNET Mandatory Transmits outbound traffic to your user-configured proxy servers and downloads blocklist updates.
Network State ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Mandatory Monitors Wi-Fi and Cellular transitions to maintain socket resilience without dropped packets.
Change Network State CHANGE_NETWORK_STATE Mandatory Enables socket protection to bypass the VPN tunnel for proxy outbound connections (VpnService.protect()).
Foreground Service FOREGROUND_SERVICE Mandatory Maintains continuous packet processing while switching between different applications.
